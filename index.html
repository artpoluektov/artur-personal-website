How I think

Big decisions deserve
better bets.

You cannot eliminate uncertainty from a strategic decision. You can choose how much to invest in reducing it, and in what order. That's the thinking behind my evolving Strategic Bet Framework.

“Good strategy isn't about knowing the future. It's about deciding what evidence is worth buying before you commit.”
01 / FRAME

Map

Name the opportunity, assumptions and what nobody knows.

02 / RESEARCH

Scan

Find the cheapest useful evidence before spending real money.

03 / DECIDE

Reconcile

Challenge the original belief and set the conditions to stop.

04 / VALIDATE

Test

Run the smallest meaningful test of the riskiest assumption.

05 / COMMIT

Make the play

Invest deliberately, with clear ownership and measurable gates.

The framework synthesises established ideas in real options, discovery-driven planning, experimentation and decision quality. Its value is in the discipline of applying them, not claiming to predict outcomes.
